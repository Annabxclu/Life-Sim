# 🏡 Life Sim

A tap-based life simulation game that runs in any web browser, including on your phone. Everything is in a single `index.html` file. There's nothing to install.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. To play online, turn on GitHub Pages: go to **Settings → Pages**, set the source to **Deploy from a branch**, and choose `main` and `/ (root)`.

## Features

- **Create a family**: one person or up to six family members, starting with $30,000
- **Buy a home**: 7 homes in Willow Creek and 2 in Neon City, each listing its rooms and the furniture it comes with
- **Rooms**: tap a room to use what's in it, or rename rooms (like "Mia's Room")
- **Actions by room**: Kitchen → Cook (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Dessert) or Takeout (Pizza, Chinese, Burgers, and more), plus actions for the bathroom, bedroom, living room and others
- **Build mode**: buy furniture and upgrades (⭐ to ⭐⭐⭐), place them in any room or your inventory, sell items for 50%, and build new rooms
- **Needs**: Hunger, Energy, Hygiene, Bladder, Fun and Social
- **Ages**: baby → toddler → child → teen → young adult → adult → elder, with a birthday every 7 days
- **Jobs**: 12 careers with promotions, plus school for kids and part-time jobs for teens
- **Map**: neighbors you can visit (they may say no), and places like restaurants, a gym, a bar, a park, a café and a library, each with activities and people there
- **Social**: talk to people (Friendly, Funny, Deep, Romantic, Mean), date, propose, have babies, and invite roommates
- **Pets**: dogs, cats, bunnies, hamsters and parrots, with their own needs, pet beds, toys, walks, tricks and grooming
- **Worlds and vacations**: Neon City, Sunset Shores and Pinecrest Peaks, with hotels and souvenirs
- **Bills** are due every Monday

Progress saves automatically in your browser's local storage.
